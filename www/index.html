When to seek urgent help

If you or someone else has severe chest pain, severe difficulty breathing, signs of stroke (face drooping, arm weakness, speech difficulty), severe bleeding, or is unconscious, contact local emergency services immediately. Do not use this app to assess an emergency.

Privacy reminder

Your profile is stored locally on this device. It is not sent to a doctor, family member, or cloud account. Do not enter information on a shared device unless you are comfortable with others who can access that device seeing it.
